Story Island
The Reading Adventure

Story Island needs your help! Read, explore and collect three magical Reading Crystals.

How to Play

  • PC: Use W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys to move. Click and drag to look around.
  • Tablet/Phone: Use the left joystick to move and drag on the right side to look.
  • Walk up to objects bouncing with markers.
  • Read the stories carefully to solve the challenges and earn crystals!

Teacher Corner

Target group: Year 4 primary ESL pupils.

Main skill: Reading comprehension.

Learning Focus:

  • Level 1: Finding specific information.
  • Level 2: Sequencing and vocabulary in context.
  • Level 3: Making inferences and identifying main ideas.

Suggested use: Individual practice, pair work, or teacher-led activity.

Duration: ~15–25 minutes.

Note: Results are practice indicators, not a formal reading assessment.
